# Portfolio content glossary

This glossary defines the shared language for portfolio content and claim review. It intentionally contains no implementation choices.

## Language

**Claim**: A factual, biographical, or measurable statement proposed for public display, linked to evidence and an approval state.

**Evidence**: A source that supports a Claim, such as an approved résumé record, owner confirmation, public paper, or public repository artifact.

**Public-safe**: Appropriate to publish without exposing private, confidential, proprietary, or security-sensitive information.

**Approved Claim**: A Claim whose wording, Evidence, and public-safety state the owner has accepted for publication.

**Content Record**: A typed portfolio entry, such as a Profile, Experience, Project, Research record, Publication, Highlight, or Link.

**Project**: A completed body of work presented with an approved scope, personal contribution, evidence, and limitations.

**Research in Progress**: Active research whose questions and methods may be public, while incomplete or unsupported outcomes remain unpublished.

**Workstream**: A bounded line of investigation inside a Research in Progress record, with its own question, method, status, evidence, and limitations.

**Publication**: A scholarly record represented with canonical title, authorship context, venue or preprint status, year, and public link.

**Verification Date**: The most recent date on which a Claim's wording and Evidence were checked for continued accuracy.
